About the founder

Meet The Founder

Gilbert Zeron Banda — Founder, Teck Xedi · Third-year student, Agricultural Extension

Gilbert Zeron Banda is a young entrepreneur and visionary builder from Malawi. He founded Teck Xedi with the belief that technology should solve everyday problems and create opportunities for future generations.

His mission is not only to build software but also to create lasting impact across education, agriculture, healthcare, business and society. Alongside building Teck Xedi, Gilbert is a third-year student pursuing a degree in Agricultural Extension — a background that shapes how closely he ties his technology ideas to Malawi’s real, everyday needs.

  1. The problem

    Everyday challenges that can be automated, or turned into software that makes the work easier.

  2. The idea

    Gilbert’s experience at LUANAR has shown him how much better software can be built today than ten years ago.

  3. The vision

    Make more in the golden era of AI, to the benefit of humanity.
